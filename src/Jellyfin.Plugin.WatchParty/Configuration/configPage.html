Watch Party

Your Discord server votes to pause, resume, skip, stop and pick what plays. The bot runs inside Jellyfin and only connects out to Discord, so you don't need to open ports or set up anything else.

  • 1

    Connect your Discord bot

    Paste a bot token below and press Save.

  • 2

    Add the bot to your server

    Once the bot is connected, an invite link appears here.

    Invite the bot to your Discord server

  • 3

    Play something on the screen you share

    Start playing in any Jellyfin app. It becomes the host screen.

How to make a bot (about 2 minutes)
  1. Open discord.com/developers/applications and click New Application. The name you pick is the bot's name.
  2. Installation page: set Install Link to None and click Save Changes.
  3. Bot page: turn off Public Bot (so strangers can't add it), then click Reset Token → Copy.
  4. Paste the token above and click Save. Step 1 turns green and step 2 shows the invite link.
